# Policy Document: Student Eligibility for Off-Campus Internships (DEMO / SYNTHETIC)

## Clause 1.1: Academic Standing & CGPA Threshold
To be eligible for an off-campus internship during summer breaks or academic semesters, undergraduate students must satisfy the following minimum academic criteria:
- **Minimum CGPA**: The student must maintain a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of **2.5 / 4.0** (or **6.5 / 10.0**) at the time of application submission.
- **Active Backlogs**: The student must have **zero (0) active backlogs** across all previous semesters. Pending re-examinations or unresolved failing grades automatically disqualify the application until cleared.

## Clause 1.2: Semester & Credit Standing
- **Semester Prerequisite**: Undergraduate engineering students must have completed at least **5 full semesters** of coursework before commencing an internship.
- **Credit Requirement**: A minimum of **100 earned academic credits** must be logged in the university portal prior to starting the internship period.

## Clause 1.3: Internship Duration Limits
- **Summer Internship**: Maximum allowed duration is **12 weeks** between May 15 and August 15.
- **Semester Internship**: Extended semester internships (up to **24 weeks**) require additional approval from the Academic Performance Committee and course credit mapping.
