# Policy Document: Required Supporting Documents for NOC Requests (DEMO / SYNTHETIC)

## Clause 3.1: Internship Offer Letter Checklist
The uploaded Internship Offer Letter must be a PDF document containing all of the following explicit fields:
1. **Company Letterhead & Logo**: Official branding of the hiring organization.
2. **Student Full Name**: Must match official university enrollment records.
3. **Internship Job Role / Title**: e.g., "Software Engineering Intern".
4. **Exact Start Date and End Date**: Must specify day, month, and year (e.g. 01 June 2026 to 31 August 2026).
5. **Stipend / Compensation Terms**: Declared monthly stipend or explicit unpaid designation.
6. **HR Signature & Official Contact**: Signature of HR Manager/Recruiter with corporate email address.

## Clause 3.2: Parent / Guardian Consent Form
- A signed **Parent/Guardian Consent Form** is mandatory for all off-campus internships.
- The form must confirm awareness of the internship location, dates, and safety guidelines.

## Clause 3.3: Document Format & Upload Limits
- Accepted formats: **PDF only**.
- Maximum file size per document: **10 MB**.
