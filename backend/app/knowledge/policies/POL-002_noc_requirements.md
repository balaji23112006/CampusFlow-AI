# Policy Document: No Objection Certificate (NOC) Issuance Rules (DEMO / SYNTHETIC)

## Clause 2.1: Purpose of University NOC
A No Objection Certificate (NOC) is an official university legal instrument confirming that:
1. The student is in good standing with the university.
2. The university permits the student to participate in the specified internship during the declared dates.
3. The host organization is granted permission to host the student on site or remotely.

## Clause 2.2: Company Verification Criteria
NOC certificates will only be issued for internships hosted by recognized legal entities:
- Host companies must have a valid Corporate Identification Number (CIN) or tax registration ID.
- Internships offered by unverified personal accounts or unregistered entities will not receive an NOC.

## Clause 2.3: Work Mode Regulations
- **On-Site Internships**: Require physical location confirmation and health insurance coverage declaration.
- **Remote / Hybrid Internships**: Require explicit confirmation in the offer letter that remote working equipment and supervisor oversight are provided.
