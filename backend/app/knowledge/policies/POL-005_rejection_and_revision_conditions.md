# Policy Document: Common Rejection & Revision Conditions (DEMO / SYNTHETIC)

## Clause 5.1: Automatic Rejection Conditions
An Internship NOC request will be **automatically rejected** under the following conditions:
1. **Low CGPA**: Student CGPA is below the mandatory threshold of 2.5 / 4.0.
2. **Active Backlogs**: Student has 1 or more uncleared failing grades (active backlogs).
3. **Invalid Offer Letter**: Offer letter is missing, illegible, or from an unregistered entity.

## Clause 5.2: Action Required / Revision Triggers
A request status will be updated to **ACTION_REQUIRED** (Request Changes) if:
1. **Missing Parent Consent**: The parent/guardian consent form is missing or unsigned.
2. **Date Conflicts**: Internship start date overlaps with university mandatory mid-term or end-term examinations.
3. **Incomplete HR Details**: Offer letter lacks verifiable corporate email address or official signature.
